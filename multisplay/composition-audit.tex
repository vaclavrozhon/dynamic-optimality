\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The proposed bound
\[
 \operatorname{cost}_{\rm MS}(X)
 =O\!\left((\operatorname{OPT}(X)+n)
       (1+\log^{(3)}n)^2(1+\log^{(4)}n)^2\right)
\]
passes this independent hand audit as a composition of the stated local
Splay and Cha-Cha proof dependencies. This audit did not
independently reprove the two core manuscripts and does not establish
a new Lean theorem. In particular the mixed-forest comparison must be
proved from the accounting interfaces; it is not a black-box consequence
of the fixed-universe Splay theorem.

\paragraph{The precise reduction.}
With $H=\lceil\log_2(n+1)\rceil$, put
$\lambda=\Theta(1+\log\log H)$ and
$g(H)=O((1+\log\log H)(1+\log\log\log H)^2)$.
The four required statements have matching domains and conventions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The endpoint embedding bounds $m+S+F_{\rm act}$ by
      $O(\operatorname{OPT}(X)+n)$ for the actual final-switch convention.
\item The mixed word $W_{\rm MS}(X)$ uses at most $6S$ structural
      operations, starts on exactly $n$ original keys, and every
      intermediate component has at most $H$ keys.
\item Its balanced Cha-Cha comparator has cost
      $C=O(n+\lambda(S+F_{\rm act}))$ and obeys exactly that word's
      pivot deletions and raw root joins.
\item The persistent forest Splay comparison costs
      $O((C+n+6S)g(H))$. Its global initialization is charged once.
\end{enumerate}
Substitution gives $O((\operatorname{OPT}+n)\lambda g(H))$.
The factors multiply; none of these statements alone permits cancellation
of either leading $\log\log H$ factor.

\paragraph{Normal-form interfaces checked directly.}
The audit read the actual statements and proofs in the following modules:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{OfflineHeavyJoin.lean} and \texttt{OfflineHeavySplit.lean};
\item \texttt{OfflineRibRootOperations.lean};
\item \texttt{OfflineRibBoundedSteps.lean};
\item \texttt{OfflineRestrictedMacros.lean} and \texttt{OfflineRibbedTrace.lean}.
\end{itemize}
The heavy root operations retain the two child representations and
have support $O(\log^2H)$ with constant amortized allowance $115$.
Rib root operations have allowance $448$; reorientation to either side
has allowance $896$ and retains the represented source tree.
The raw join geometry needed between those interfaces is explicitly
present in the proof of \texttt{exists\_boundedService}: a left child
oriented \texttt{true} and right child oriented \texttt{false} give
minor-rib slack $19$. Supported-path conversion raises this to at most
$20$, preserves height within the support allowance, and uses rotations
at depth at most one. These facts support a single fixed capacity $H$
over all forest components. The additional forest datatype, potential
sum, and transport through order isomorphisms remain hand composition.

\paragraph{Accounting at a raw join.}
Giving the new common pivot rank zero and increasing both old child
rank functions by one preserves every internal rank difference, heavy
path, interval history, and pass counter. Each old component has exactly
one root heavy path. It becomes one new heap child of rank gap one.
At most two constant-size interval insertions and value adjustments
are required. The disappearing pivot's growing slots must be converted
and retired at deletion; otherwise reinsertion could violate the
per-creator bound. The detailed forest note includes this retirement
and retains all surviving child histories. A sum of per-lifetime
initialization terms is neither used nor justified.

\paragraph{The exact common word.}
After splitting the switched key $y$, the two guard splays are precisely
ordinary root splays in its daughter components. The nearest lower and
upper reference ancestors are determined by the path prefix, independent
of auxiliary shapes. The raw joins reconstruct exactly the three-splay
multi-splay result. Missing guards and physically empty tails are valid
empty-component cases. The initial physical search can be charged to
these splays: its two exit-gap neighbors are the pivot and an ancestor
guard, and bottom-up work below an initial auxiliary leaves that
auxiliary unchanged until its switch.

\paragraph{The comparator transport.}
The suffix representation needs one administrative seam: its all-left
boundary spine is initially separate from the leftmost genuine
subtree. Removing and restoring this seam uses constant many balanced
operations, and a suffix request changes at most one boundary preference.
Only genuine owner bits enter the flip ledger. For a fixed owner row,
the genuine writes are obtained from the analytical actual-order word
by deleting writes made while that owner lies above the selected
component. All histories travel through the splice. Initialized binary
variation therefore bounds the comparator's genuine flips.

The guard exposure uses actual external gaps, not the names of abstract
routing labels. After exposing the last two prefix gaps, their physical
search paths contain $\ell,y,r$ whenever these guards exist. This is
why the bounded hull contains the pivots mandated by the common word.
The old tail remains inside its original common component throughout
preparation. The incoming component is prepared through its own root
handle. Reconfiguring the two rooted skeletons to the exact split/join
checkpoints preserves the opaque frontier intervals, after which each
resulting skeleton is separately reconfigured to the prescribed
Cha-Cha state. No rotation crosses a current component boundary.

\paragraph{Scope that must remain explicit.}
The reference is height-optimal; this gives $2^H=O(n)$ in the endpoint
initialization estimate. Padding adds only virtual routing nodes and
retains all original external gaps as distinct active atoms, including
the two null gaps beside a real leaf. Thus there are no physical dummy
keys or extra prescribed splits. The comparator uses the augmented
pointer-BST model of the local Cha-Cha proof, with free computation
from read headers. No word-RAM running-time claim follows. The exact
$O(\log^{(3)}n)$ bound for standard multi-splay is not established.

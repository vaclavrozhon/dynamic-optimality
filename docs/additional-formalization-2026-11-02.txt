Additional formalization requested on 2 November 2026
===================================================

All targets below are formalized and included in the completed full build
and axiom audit recorded in verification-additional-2026-11-02.txt. Their
public scope is documented in FORMALIZATION.md. The requested numerical
lower bounds for the Splay interface (item 5 of the discussion) are excluded.

Main targets
------------

1. Two-level Cha-Cha with ordinary bottom-up Splay auxiliary trees and
   competitive factor O(L_3(n)), using the shared search/rotation model.
   A local access lemma or a conditional representation interface alone
   does not complete this target.

2. The explicit Theta(L_3(n)) separation between the robust rectangle
   bound and the fixed-reference primary/depth-alternation counter.
   Both counters must be evaluated on the same actual request family.

3. Funnel bounds, with occurrences distinguished and repeated keys allowed:
   - an arbitrary two-source shuffle W of X and Y satisfies
     F(W) + |W| = O(OPT(X) + OPT(Y));
   - convex-interval composition satisfies
     F(W) <= F(template) + sum_i F(part_i) + 9|W|.
   The optimum is the existing repository optimum.

4. Rowwise nearest-common-ancestor closure in an arbitrary fixed reference
   BST preserves arboral satisfaction. A nonempty row of r keys grows to
   at most 2r-1 keys. The keys occurring in any closed time interval are
   also closed after this construction. Record the separate finite
   obstruction to adjoining fixed ancestor guards.

6. A comparison normal form of height O(log n), cost O(OPT+n), bounded
   minor ribs, and constant-depth rotations. Constants are uniform in
   universe size and request word. A conditional weighted-stack interface
   does not complete the target.

Further results explicitly mentioned in the discussion
------------------------------------------------------

- The entropy residual strengthening of depth alternation, distinguishing
  the deterministic lower-bound assertion from the fixed-histogram
  random-order expectation.
- Ordered subsets admitting cheap adjacent-color moves, and the resulting
  bound for shuffles of nearest-neighbor walks.
- The precise MultiSplay potential/guard counterexamples; these obstruct
  specified analyses, not the competitive theorem.
- A causal online geometric-to-physical-BST conversion with constant
  factor. Offline reconstruction is insufficient for this claim.

Verification and integration
----------------------------

The final repository-wide audit passed for all 2,507 registered modules,
69,472 declarations, and 406 explicit endpoint diagnostics. The
following targeted checks were also completed during development:

- Item 1: ChaCha.Paper20261030.SplayAuxiliaryAll builds. Its public
  All.third_log_competitive proves O(L_3(n)) with actual ordinary Splay
  auxiliaries, original keys, the shared OPT, and an arbitrary supplied
  initial BST. The ordinary-operation certificates are retained by
  All.ordinary_active and Natural.ordinary.
- Item 2: ChaCha.Separation.Explicit builds. The same sparse repeated
  bit-reversal family has m <= F0 <= 4m, where F0=m+P+D, and
  L_3(n)*F0 <= 8*RIR <= 32*L_3(n)*F0. The proof uses the full midpoint
  depth reference. docs/separation-formalization-notes.txt records its
  minor change of parameters relative to the manuscript.
- Item 3: all 52 new Funnel modules build. ConvexComposition has the exact
  additive 9m for arbitrary monotone interval labels. ShuffleOPT proves
  F(W)+|W| <= 360639*(OPT(X)+OPT(Y)), including repeated keys and overlapping
  source alphabets, with no universe-size additive term. Both occurrence
  embedding and inductive interleaving interfaces are exported. The five
  principal endpoint axiom checks contain only the accepted axioms.
- Item 4: DynamicOptimality.ReferenceClosure builds, including the actual
  execution transformation, the closed-time-interval theorem, and the
  finite ancestor-guard counterexample.
- Item 6: Splay.Accounting.LogHeightNormalForm builds. Its
  exists_structuralLogHeightComparisonTrace bounds every intermediate
  tree's height by 30025000*(ceil(log_2(n+1))+1), every rotation's depth by
  1, the minor ribs by 20, and the total cost by 400000000*(OPT+n).
  additiveStructuralHypothesis discharges the existing shared structural
  hypothesis with constants independent of n and of the request word.
- Entropy residual: ExpectationEntropy builds with the complete uniform
  fixed-histogram probability space, exact primary expectation, actual
  weighted-median auxiliary reference, and the combined entropy/OPT
  endpoint. The same reference is chosen before the random order and
  satisfies the deterministic 519*OPT bound on every permutation.
- MultiSplay obstructions: IntervalProjection and
  ClassicalPotentialObstruction build. Both use actual ordinary Splay
  transcripts. The latter embeds an unbounded family into complete
  physical BST states with persistent inventories and the actual ledger.
  Its scope is arbitrary legal initialized states and a specified local
  potential inequality, not a competitive lower bound.
- Monotone projection: ColoredWalks.ProjectionExecution builds. Actual
  geometric executions project between finite universes without increasing
  cost; merged keys and unused target keys are allowed.
- Online geometric simulation: CausalInitialized and GreedyInstance build.
  One physical online BST is fixed before all future words and starts from
  each supplied initial tree. It costs at most 387*n+381*geometricCost.
  The concrete split-tree system, causal row extraction, and all-prefix
  potential argument are implemented. Targeted endpoint axioms contain
  only the standard three axioms.
- Colored walks: the static colored embedding, dense observed-key
  encoding, actual persistent-finger walks, and the final projection to
  the shared OPT build. MultiFinger.simulationBound discharges the complete
  physical simulator with constant 1024. ColoredWalks.Shuffle then proves
  OPT <= 292864*m*(1+floor(log_2(k+1)))^2 without an additive universe term,
  and an actual supplied-initial-BST run at twice that bound plus 12*n.
  The final public endpoint is unconditional.

New results use the existing tree, cost, geometry, and online interfaces.
The targeted checks were followed by the full registered-module build and
axiom audit before publishing the completed results. The untracked research
notes predate this work and are not included in this commit.

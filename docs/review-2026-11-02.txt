Lean library review and refactoring — 2 November 2026
===================================================

Scope
-----

The structural review covered every registered Lean module, its compiled
imports, and the complete declaration dependency graph. The baseline at
1acfcf5 had 2,228 tracked Lean files (355,158 lines), of which 2,224 were
registered mathematical modules. The remaining files were audit/export tools.

A comment-aware source scan found no explicit sorry/admit, axiom or opaque
declarations, unsafe declarations, or native_decide in that baseline.
This source scan is supplementary: the compiled axiom audit is the relevant
check for logical dependencies, including dependencies introduced by tactics.

Manual mathematical review concentrated on the shared definitions, public
algorithm statements, initialization, online transitions, comparator interfaces,
and final numerical reductions. This is not a claim to have manually reread
every line of every proof. Kernel checking and whole-library axiom coverage
complement that review; neither alone establishes that a definition models
the intended algorithm.

Findings and changes
--------------------

1. Audit coverage

The old AuditAll import closure contained 2,151 of the 2,224 registered
modules. The other 73 modules contained 2,223 declarations. A further 81
loaded declarations, including five handwritten List lemmas and generated
helpers, fell outside its namespace filter. This accounts for the difference
between the graph's 63,307 declarations and the audit's 61,003 targets.
Some omitted targets could still be visited as dependencies of audited ones.
This was a coverage gap, not an identified false theorem or rejected proof.

The new tools/audit.py runner builds all registered modules and constructs
one import driver from compiler metadata. tools/lean_audit/Audit.lean checks
that every registered module was loaded, then selects declarations by their
actual source module. It includes private and generated declarations and
local declarations in other namespaces. All explicit endpoint diagnostics
from both historical root audit lists are retained. One complete pass
replaces the two overlapping namespace scans in make audit. The old root
files remain usable as narrower direct checks.

2. One generic MultiSplay argument

GlobalPreferredGenericBound now proves admissible_egTargetFactor: the concrete
refined Splay factor satisfies the persistent search/split/join interface
required by the generic theorem. The same coefficient function works for
every capacity, with domination constant 16*168.

The actual-forest bound, the one-time initialization argument, and the exact
logarithmic-capacity theorem now specialize the generic argument instead of
repeating it for the concrete factor. ComparatorEstimate is defined once in
its own module to keep imports acyclic. Its fully qualified name and defining
condition are unchanged. Existing public theorem names and types are retained.
The fixed algorithm, its cost, its comparator construction, and its online
choices are unchanged. Existential numerical witnesses in the reorganized
proofs need not be the same witnesses as before.

An independent source comparison found all 20 pre-existing declaration
headers in the changed mathematical modules unchanged after whitespace
normalization; the moved ComparatorEstimate definition is byte-identical.
The internal import direction changes: InitializedGenericBound no longer
imports its concrete specialization. A client that obtained concrete results
incidentally through that generic module should import
InitializedComparatorBound or CompetitiveLogHeight explicitly. The documented
public result entry points retain their previous declarations.

3. Consistent public entry points

DynamicOptimality.Results.splay_refined exposes the existing refined Splay
theorem beside the simpler one. DynamicOptimality.MultiSplayBound now exports
the exact logarithmic-capacity result as multisplay_logarithmic_competitive,
while retaining multisplay_competitive for its older shifted formulation.

4. Mathematical boundaries retained

Competitive fixes one constant before the universe, supplied initial tree,
and request sequence. Every comparison uses the same attained OPT.
Cha-Cha and classical MultiSplay certify ordinary rotations and the initial
search depth, on exactly the original physical keys, with initialization paid
once. Their concrete endpoints retain no unproved comparator premise.

Generic MultiSplay still requires the persistent split/join interface; a bound
for query-only Splay is not substituted for it. Greedy remains a geometric
online algorithm. Finite cost equivalence of the five models does not claim
the deferred online geometry-to-BST conversion.

5. Deliberately retained structure

At the baseline, the six inspected result endpoints collectively reached
23,257 local declarations. The other 40,050 declarations include generated
helpers, reusable results, and separate controller developments. Lack of a
path from a chosen endpoint is not grounds for deleting those modules.

Small remaining duplications, such as two proofs identifying an indexed
tree's inorder list with List.range, do not justify changing foundational
interfaces in this pass. The refactor targets the duplicated final MultiSplay
argument and the concrete audit-coverage problem.

Proof graph
-----------

The viewer has prominent Modules / Declarations / Neighborhood controls,
explicit zoom buttons, and shortcuts to the public results. Stars identify
main algorithm statements and their proof modules; a diamond distinguishes
the supporting model-equivalence theorem. Hovering highlights every incident
edge in the current filtered view, including edges omitted from the counted
background drawing sample. These markers are navigation metadata, not new
Lean declarations or assumptions.
